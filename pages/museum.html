The Experience

A Living Museum

The 95th Bomb Group Heritage Association operates the museum from a number of faithfully restored buildings on one of the communal sites of the original 95th airbase. The centrepiece is the NCO's social club — the Red Feather Club itself — restored to its 1940s appearance and filled with authentic artefacts, memorabilia, and stories.

  • 🪖
    Restored Buildings
    Faithfully restored wartime structures, including the original NCO's social club, exactly as they would have appeared during the war years.
  • 🎖️
    Authentic Artefacts
    Uniforms, equipment, personal items, photographs and documents — many donated by veterans and their families.
  • 🎓
    Educational Visits
    We welcome schools and educational groups throughout the year. Our experienced volunteers bring the history to life with guided tours and first-hand accounts.
  • 🌐
    Virtual Museum Tour
    Can't visit in person? Explore the museum from home with our virtual tour — a 360° walkthrough of the restored buildings.

Remembrance

The Memorials

The site at Horham includes a number of permanent memorials to the men of the 95th Bomb Group and the wider Eighth Air Force. These memorials provide a place of quiet reflection, remembrance, and gratitude.

The Roll of Honour

All those who gave their lives in service with the 95th are remembered here.

The Memorial Garden

A peaceful garden of remembrance at the heart of the site, beautifully maintained by our volunteers.

Permanent Plaques

Individual and squadron memorial plaques, many placed by veterans' families.
