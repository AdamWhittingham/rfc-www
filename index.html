A Place of Memory & Living History

The 95th Bomb Group Heritage Association preserves the buildings, stories, and legacy of the men who flew from Horham — the only Eighth Air Force group to receive the Distinguished Unit Citation three times.

01

Our Past

From the first mission in May 1943 to the first daylight raid on Berlin, discover the extraordinary story of the 95th Bomb Group and the men who flew for freedom.

Read the History
02

The Museum

Step inside a faithfully restored 1940s NCO's social club. Visitors say it's like going back in time — 1940s memorabilia, artefacts, and stories brought back to life.

Visit the Museum

Events & Open Days

Regular open days, 1940s dances, living history weekends, school visits and special events throughout the year. Something for everyone.

95th Bomb Group Heritage Association
